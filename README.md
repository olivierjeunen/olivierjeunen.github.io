Olivier Jeunen is an independent researcher and machine learning science contractor collaborating with Booking.com on advertising and relevance.
He obtained his PhD in Computer Science from the University of Antwerp in 2021 and previously held research positions at Aampe, ShareChat, Amazon, Spotify, Meta and Criteo.
His research focuses on recommender systems, online advertising and experimentation, with particular emphasis on causal inference and off-policy evaluation and learning.
His research and reviewing contributions have received multiple awards.
He served as Industry Co-Chair for ECIR 2024, RecSys 2025, and UMAP 2027; and as an LBR & Demo Co-Chair for RecSys 2027.
